  • Understanding Lung Cancer: A Quick Overview - Lung cancer is a major health issue that occurs when abnormal cells grow uncontrollably in the lungs, forming tumors. Primarily caused by smoking, it can also affect non-smokers through factors like secondhand smoke, environmental pollutants, and genetic risks. Lung cancer is categorized into two main types: Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) and Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC), each requiring tailored treatments like surgery, chemotherapy, and newer methods like targeted therapy. Early symptoms include persistent cough, chest pain, and shortness of breath, making regular screening crucial for at-risk individuals. Prevention through lifestyle choices, reducing smoke exposure, and screening can help mitigate lung cancer’s impact.
